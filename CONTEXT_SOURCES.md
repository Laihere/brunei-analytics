## Notes on specific sources

**Sultanate.com reprint of the Borneo Bulletin (checked 16 Oct 2026).** The page ends with the
credit line "– Borneo Bulletin (27th May 2011)", so it names the original publisher and date.
Sultanate.com is an independent Brunei news site, online since at least December 1998
(Internet Archive), with a dated news archive going back to 2004. It has no "about" page, so
who runs it, and whether it had permission to reprint, couldn't be confirmed. Borneo Bulletin's
current website search finds nothing from 2011, and the Internet Archive has no Borneo
Bulletin page near that date, so the original isn't available online. The reference cites the
Borneo Bulletin as the publisher and says where it was read.

The two sources differ on the date: ASEAN (2016) says the blueprint was launched in February
2011; the Borneo Bulletin reports its official launch ceremony in May 2011. The page gives both.

**Peer-reviewed sources for case study 05 (searched 16 Oct 2026).** No peer-reviewed study of
Brunei's food processing industry in 2004–2013 was found, so the page uses two institutional
reports (tier 2) and says so.

**Uses beyond 05.** `searca2016` is also used in case study 06, where the page compares its
statement ("poultry and eggs … nearly 100 percent self-sufficiency since 1994") with the
Department's own figures: true for eggs; chicken meat passed 90% only in 2001. `khut2026` is
also used in 06 for chicken and eggs above 100% and beef "substantially lower" (p. 2).
